\title[A pillowcase action complex for surgeries]{A pillowcase action complex $C(K)$ and a filtered mapping cone\\ for the Chern--Simons filtered instanton homology of $1/n$ surgeries}
\date{October 2026}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Let $K$ be a knot in an integer homology sphere $Y$ whose Alexander polynomial has no roots on the unit circle and whose irreducible $\SU(2)$ representation variety is a smooth compact $1$-manifold.  We define a complex $C(K)$ from the image $\Gam$ of this variety in the pillowcase, together with a Chern--Simons action, a grading, and the two instanton complexes $\CI(Y)$ and $\CI(Y_0(K),w)$ as an $A_\infty$-module over the two-line algebra of the pillowcase.  We give a mapping cone formula expressing the Chern--Simons filtered irreducible instanton complex of $Y_{1/n}(K)$ as an iterated cone, with the filtration offsets $(n-1)/4$ computed as the symplectic action of the corner morphisms, and the generator-level data (Chern--Simons values, gradings) computed by Kirk--Klassen integrals along $\Gam$.  The parts that are proved are separated from the parts that are conjectural (the identification of differentials with pillowcase bigons).  A workflow with concrete numerical tests is specified.
\end{abstract}
