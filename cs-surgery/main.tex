\title[A pillowcase action complex for surgeries]{A pillowcase action complex $C(K)$ and a filtered mapping cone\\ for the Chern--Simons filtered instanton homology of $1/n$ surgeries}
\date{October 2026}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
Let $K$ be a knot in an integer homology sphere $Y$ whose Alexander polynomial has no roots on the unit circle and whose irreducible $\SU(2)$ representation variety is a smooth compact $1$-manifold.  We define a complex $C(K)$ from the image $\Gam$ of this variety in the pillowcase, together with a Chern--Simons action, a grading, and the two instanton complexes $\CI(Y)$ and $\CI(Y_0(K),w)$ as an $A_\infty$-module over the two-line algebra of the pillowcase.  We give a mapping cone formula for the Chern--Simons filtered irreducible instanton complex of $Y_{1/n}(K)$: $\CI(Y_{1/n})\cong\Cone\big(\CI(Y_{1/(n-1)})\to\CI(Y_0,w)\big)$ as complexes, with the Chern--Simons value of each generator given exactly by a Kirk--Klassen integral along $\Gam$ plus a signed area $\delta_n(x)$ in the pillowcase.  The constant-shift version (which is what the level bounds of Daemi--Lidman--Miller Eismeier control) is shown to be non-exact by the figure-eight knot, and is used only as a check.  The exact statement is obtained from Floer's own proof of the triangle via collar holonomy perturbations, matched with Lagrangian surgery in the pillowcase.  What is proved, what is numerically verified (trefoil, figure-eight), and what remains conjectural are separated, and a workflow is specified.
\end{abstract}
