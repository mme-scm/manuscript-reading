\section{Tests}\label{sec:tests}
%======================================================================
Scripts, outputs and plots are in \texttt{numerics/}.  Ground truth: the Fintushel--Stern values
$\cs=-e^2/(4a_1a_2a_3)$ on $\Sigma(a_1,a_2,a_3)$ oriented as the link of the singularity.  Orientations were fixed
independently of $\cs$: $S^3_{1/n}(\text{RHT})=-\Sigma(2,3,6n-1)$, $S^3_{-1/n}(\text{RHT})=+\Sigma(2,3,6n+1)$, and
$S^3_{+1}(4_1)=+\Sigma(2,3,7)$.  Throughout, $\cs_{\rm KK}(Z)=-\cs_{\rm FS}(Z)$: the Kirk--Klassen normalisation is the
negative of Fintushel--Stern's on every manifold tested.

\subsection{Right-handed trefoil: Proposition~\ref{prop:action} holds exactly}
$\pi_1=\langle x,y\mid x^2=y^3\rangle$, $\mu=y^{-1}x$, $\lambda=x^2\mu^{-6}$.  The irreducible curve is the segment
$b=\tfrac12-6a$, $a\in[\tfrac1{12},\tfrac5{12}]$, from the reducible point $(\tfrac1{12},0)$ to $(\tfrac5{12},-2)$, and
$f_\Gamma(a)=6(a-\tfrac1{12})^2$ measured from the trivial connection along $b=0$.  On $S^3_{1/n}$ the formula gives
the closed form $\cs=-(6m-6n+1)^2/(24(6n-1))$, which is $-e^2/(4P)$ in disguise.

\subsection{Right-handed trefoil: the naive corner-offset identity is false}
$\Gamma\cap\{b=\tfrac12\}$ consists of $a=\tfrac16$ and $a=\tfrac13$.  No reading of the action, no sign, and no offset
in $\{0,\tfrac14,\tfrac12,\tfrac34\}$ makes $\{\cs(S^3_{1/2})\}$ equal to $\{\cs(S^3_1)\}\cup\{A(x)+\text{offset}\}$:
the denominators are $264$, $120$ and $24$.  This refutes the generator-level reading of the cone that the first
draft of Theorem~\ref{thm:cone}(c) contained, and it is as expected: a filtered homotopy equivalence preserves the
filtered homotopy type, not the levels of generators.  A side observation: on the line $b\in\tfrac12+\Z$ the action
is only defined modulo $\tfrac14$ (changing the auxiliary $d$ in $Q$ shifts it by $b^2=\tfrac14$), which is the
pillowcase form of the quarter-integer ambiguity of instanton energies on cobordisms with $w^2\ne0$.

\subsection{Figure-eight knot}
With $\pi_1=\langle x,y\mid wx=yw\rangle$, $w=x^{-1}yxy^{-1}$, Riley's parametrisation gives the irreducible circle
$\Gamma$ over $a\in[\tfrac16,\tfrac13]$, symmetric under $b\mapsto-b$, with folds of $a$ at $(\tfrac16,\tfrac12)$ and
$(\tfrac13,\tfrac12)$, one transverse double point at $(\tfrac14,0)$, and \emph{no branch points} (the restriction
map is an immersion; $\min|(\dot a,\dot b)|=0.226$).  The lift of one circuit translates by $(0,\mp2)$ and
$\oint2b\,da=\tfrac13$, so $f_\Gamma$ does \emph{not} descend to $\Gamma$: it is a section of the Kirk--Klassen circle
bundle, and the transition term makes the corrected period $-1$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:period}).
The absolute constant from the $SL(2,\C)$ path of Andersen--Hansen is $c=-\tfrac16$, and then

\subsection{Figure-eight knot: the exact cone (Theorem~\ref{thm:exact})}

$\pm\tfrac14$, for $n=1,2,3$.  Minimal position: $2=0+2$, $4=2+2$, $6=4+2$.  An explicit equivariant isotopy
(vertex moving linearly to the midpoint of $m_1m_2$) keeps the count constant through $400$ steps; no
birth--death.  With $A_{\rm cone}$ as in Proposition~\ref{prop:zigzag},

\subsection{Figure-eight knot: Farey triads (Theorem~\ref{thm:allslopes})}
All twelve bundle combinations of the triads $(1,1/0)\to2$, $(1/2,1)\to2/3$, $(2,1)\to3/2$ pass: zero flux
(triangles of $\omega$-area $\pm\tfrac14$), the balanced line is the one predicted by the parity rule
$\varepsilon_\gamma=\varepsilon_\alpha+\varepsilon_\beta+1$, counts are additive ($2|q|$ points on slope $p/q$, as $\Gam$ has class $(0,2)$),
the equivariant isotopy has no birth--death, and $A(x')=A_{\rm cone}(x)+\int_{R(x)}\omega$ holds to $3\cdot10^{-10}$.
The offsets $(\kappa_\alpha,\kappa_\beta)$ found: $(\tfrac14,0)$ for $L^0_1,L^0_{1/0}\to L^w_2$; $(0,\tfrac12)$ for
$L^0_1,L^w_{1/0}\to L^0_2$; $(\tfrac14,0)$, $(0,\tfrac14)$, $(\tfrac14,0)$, $(0,\tfrac34)$ for the four $2/3$ cases;
$(0,\tfrac14)$, $(0,\tfrac12)$, $(\tfrac12,0)$, $(0,\tfrac14)$ for $3/2$.  As the numerics agent observed, once
$\kappa$ is defined by matching primitives at the midpoints the action identity is Stokes' theorem; the content of the
test is the parity rule, zero flux, minimal position, the bijection, and the independence of $\kappa$ from the
choice of corner lift (translating $m$ by a lattice vector leaves $\kappa$ unchanged mod $\Z$).

\emph{Flag.}  $L^w_2$ and $L^w_{2/3}$ pass through the transverse double point $(\tfrac14,0)$ of $\Gam$, which is also
the image of the abelian representation with $a=\tfrac14$: two irreducible generators and one reducible share a
pillowcase image.  The generator count is unaffected (each branch is transverse), but any bigon model must treat
this coincidence; the reducible is nondegenerate by Lemma~\ref{lem:red}.  On the torus the reducible counts add,
$p_\gamma=p_\alpha+p_\beta$; in the pillowcase they do not, because of the identifications at corners.
