# Rules for any strategy (binding)

(a) **No energy estimates beyond positivity.**
- No conjecture may be an energy estimate equivalent to the drop being proved.
- The only energy inputs allowed are:
  - energy >= 0;
  - energy > 0 on simply connected cobordisms, or more generally whenever no flat connection contributes.
- Exact drops must come from non-energy terms in the level formula:
  - b^+;
  - i_0;
  - rho of flat connections on middle ends;
  - flat contributions on cobordisms that are not simply connected.

(b) **Nonzero differentials.**
- Everything must work when the differentials are nonzero.
- State the filtered-algebra lemmas for filtered complexes in general, using barcodes and boundary depth, not only for complexes with zero differential.

(c) **The exact triangles may be assumed; their existence is not the difficulty.** You must state precisely:
- which triangles you use;
- which bundles are summed;
- which middle terms appear. For example, working invariantly on the double cover branched along S, the outer terms are the sum over both bundles, and the middle term is the theory of S^3 plus the theory of (S^3, K) with traceless holonomy;
- the level of every map and homotopy, derived from the level formula.

(d) **The target is a drop with a consistent sign**, in one of two forms:
- mixed: m_1, m_2 >= 1/16, or > 0;
- both-bundle: min(lambda_0, lambda_1)(Y_2) - min(lambda_0, lambda_1)(Y_{-2}) >= 1/16, or > 0.

"> 0" suffices for the cosmetic application. Explain how 1/16 enters.

(e) **Any additional input** must be either standard (cite it) or a precisely stated lemma that is NOT an energy estimate. If you cannot avoid an energy estimate, say so explicitly and stop there.
